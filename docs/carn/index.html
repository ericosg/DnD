Càrn — Combat & Reference

Barbarian 3 · Totem Warrior (Bear) · print back-to-back with the character sheet

RAGE

Bonus action to start. Lasts 1 minute (10 rounds). 3 per long rest.

The quiet old miner becomes something else entirely when someone threatens the wee one.

While raging

  • +2 damage on melee attacks using STR
  • Advantage on STR checks and STR saves (Athletics +5 with advantage)
  • Bear: resistance to everything except psychic. Halve the damage.

Rage ends when

  • 1 minute passes, or you're knocked unconscious
  • Your turn ends and you haven't attacked a hostile creature or taken damage since your last turn
  • You end it (bonus action)

Can't while raging

  • Cast spells or rituals, or concentrate
Combat Turns

Opening turn round 1

  1. Bonus action: Rage
  2. Move up to 25 ft
  3. Action: Attack with the mattock. Reckless → roll twice, take the higher. +5 to hit · 1d12+5
Enemies now have advantage on you, but you're halving nearly everything.

Raging turn rounds 2+

  1. Action: Mattock attack (Reckless if you want it) · 1d12+5
  2. Move before or after
  3. Keep it alive: attack something every turn
No bonus action to spend yet. Level 5 brings a second swing.

Can't reach it flying / far away

  1. Action: Throw a drill steel · +5 · 1d6+3 · 30/120 ft
  2. Or a rock hammer · 20/60 ft
  3. Thrown attacks still keep a rage going, but get no rage damage
Throwing at something next to you: disadvantage.

Shove or grab replaces one attack

  1. Athletics +5 (advantage raging) vs. their Athletics or Acrobatics
  2. Shove: knock prone, or push 5 ft
  3. Grapple: needs a free hand (take one off the mattock). Their speed becomes 0.
Target no more than one size larger. Prone: melee attacks against it have advantage.

Protect the wee one

  1. Stand between it and the threat
  2. Opportunity attack (reaction) when an enemy leaves your reach
  3. Rage + Bear = you are the wall
Level 14: enemies next to you have disadvantage attacking anyone else.

A friend is down

  1. Action: Medicine +3, DC 10, to stabilize
  2. Or carry them out: STR 17, carrying capacity 255 lb
Two centuries of patching up the crew after cave-ins.

Resting
Short rest (1 hour): spend hit dice. Each heals 1d12+3.
Max HP house rule: every level gives the full 12 + 3. That's 45 at level 3, and +15 per level after.
Long rest (8 hours): regain all HP, all 3 rages, and half your hit dice (min. 1).
Session Notes
Key Rules
Reckless Attack: decide on your first attack of the turn. Only STR melee attacks get advantage.
Danger Sense: advantage on DEX saves against effects you can see: traps, fireballs, falling rock.
Opportunity attack: reaction, one melee attack when an enemy leaves your reach.
Drawing weapons: one free object interaction per turn; drawing a second weapon costs an action.
Two-handed: the mattock needs both hands. You can drop it for free.
Death saves: d20 at 0 HP. 10+ success, 9− failure. Three of either decides it. Natural 20 = back up with 1 HP; natural 1 = two failures.
Darkvision 60 ft: darkness looks like dim light, in shades of grey.
Poison: advantage on saves, resistance to damage (dwarf).
Stonecunning: +3 on History about stonework. Ask the DM when stone looks wrong.
Rituals: Speak with Animals and Beast Sense take 1 action + 10 minutes as rituals. Never while raging.
